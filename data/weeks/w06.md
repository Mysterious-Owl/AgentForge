# W6 · RAGOptimizer

## What it is
RAGOptimizer upgrades CitationRAG with three retrieval improvements - HyDE query transformation, cross-encoder reranking and extractive compression - and measures whether they help. It is for engineers who want a before/after number on the same questions under the same refusal rule.

## How it works
- `retrieve()` runs two channels: `gpt-5.4-nano-2026-03-17` writes a four-sentence hypothetical answer whose embedding is searched dense-only (top 30); the raw query goes through the Week 5 hybrid search (dense + BM25 + RRF, top 30).
- The lists are interleaved by rank, deduplicated and capped at 30, so a drifted probe cannot push out the raw query's candidates.
- `BAAI/bge-reranker-base` scores all 30 pairs on CPU in one batch; the top 5 are kept.
- `compress()` keeps the 80% of body sentences with most query overlap and always keeps the `[chunk-id]` marker line.
- Symmetric gate: refuse unless the raw query or the HyDE probe clears top-1 >= 0.55 and spread >= 0.08; `gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17` writes the cited answer.
- Routes: `POST /ask`, `POST /eval`, `POST /eval/compare`, `GET /config`.

## Key decisions
- **Nano for the probe, mini for the answer** - the probe is discarded after embedding, so the cheapest model suffices.
- **Union, not HyDE-only** - drift can cost precision but not recall; the reranker resolves precision.
- **Same gate in both comparison columns** - otherwise the baseline loses false-refusal by construction; gating both means the delta measures retrieval.
- **Local CPU reranker, extractive compression** - no extra network hop.

## Results
- `/eval/compare`, 10 golden questions, baseline vs full (`week6_notebook.ipynb`): groundedness 0.80 -> 1.00, citation recall 0.80 -> 1.00, false-refusal rate 0.20 -> 0.00; citation precision 1.00 and false-answer rate 0.00 in both. The whole delta is rows #3 and #5 (directional, not statistical).
- Row #3 (`README.md`): raw query top-1 0.579 but spread 0.0676 (fails); HyDE probe 0.757 / 0.1796 (passes).
- `/eval`, full pipeline: all five metrics at best values over 10 rows.
- The cross-encoder reranks all 30 pairs in ~110 ms p95.
- `tests/`: 4 smoke tests plus 10 gate tests.
